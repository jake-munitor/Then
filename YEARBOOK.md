# Your Year on Then

## Product promise

Your Year on Then is a private annual memory artifact. It helps someone revisit what they chose to preserve without grading the year or turning relationships into performance.

The core app remains free. A user can view the recap, save a future digital edition, order a future printed edition, or do nothing.

## Content model

The existing moment model already contains the source material needed for a future Yearbook:

- `memoryDate` determines the year and chronological placement.
- `imageUrl` supplies the photograph.
- `frontText` supplies the short caption.
- `moments/{momentId}/back/details` supplies the owner's private reflection.
- `authorUid` guarantees that only the owner's moments enter their Yearbook.

Notes, keep counts, follower counts, and social activity must not be used in recap selection, ordering, or copy.

## Experience contract

- Organize moments chronologically, grouped by season or month.
- Use one consistent editorial template rather than a complex layout editor.
- Keep the preview private to the owner.
- Never automatically publish or share a recap.
- Never describe moments as best, top, most popular, or most engaging.
- Allow an empty or quiet year to remain truthful; do not manufacture milestones.

## Phased delivery

### V1: product readiness

- Preserve accurate dates, captions, images, and owner-only reflections.
- Keep the app free with no purchase UI.
- Validate that the archive remains complete and chronologically queryable.

### Future: digital preview

- Generate a private annual preview from the owner's moments.
- Allow saving a digital copy.
- Generate previews independently from any purchase decision.

### Future: print ordering

- Offer a small fixed set of hardcover and softcover formats.
- Use a print-on-demand partner for production, shipping, taxes, and status updates.
- Generate final high-resolution print files only after checkout.
- Treat printed books as physical goods; keep digital support or digital purchases compliant with each app store's payment rules.

## Revenue guardrails

- Optional one-time support may not unlock reach, visibility, badges, or social features.
- Yearbook pricing may cover design, rendering, fulfillment, support, and a sustainable margin.
- Core posting, following, notes, archive access, and reflections remain free.
- No advertising, sponsored posts, promoted placement, or sale of personal data.
